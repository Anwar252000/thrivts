Referral & Guide

Your code is how every sale gets credited to you. Here's exactly how it works.

How you get paid

1

Share your code

Give your referral code to buyers in your network — resellers, shop owners, anyone who buys wholesale vintage.

2

They enter it at sign-up

The buyer applies your code when they register. They get 5% off their first order, and from that moment they're linked to you. Your 12-month window starts on their first order.

3

You earn 5% for a year

Every order that buyer places over the next 12 months pays you a recurring 5% — tracked automatically and shown here in real time.

Important: the referral code is the only way a buyer gets credited to you. Always make sure they enter it at sign-up — no code, no link, no commission. Earnings accrue when an order is delivered and are released after the deal settles.
